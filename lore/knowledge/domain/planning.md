# Planning bounded context

`reeve-planning` owns the work hierarchy, the delivery gate, required decisions, pull-request links, sprints and goals, urgent marks, and promised-by dates. How its crate is laid out (ports, adapters, transactions, clock) is in [[general/architecture]]; this node says what the context means and what it will not do. Reason: an agent changing planning needs the language and the guarantees before the file layout.

## Ubiquitous language

The words below are bound across API, database, CLI and UI (kb/ADR-0002 R6; sprint, goal, confidence, verdict and upkeep are trial names under kb/ADR-0014 R6; urgent is added by kb/ADR-0017; attachment by kb/ADR-0022; promise, overdue and late by kb/ADR-0025). In the decision queue, an *affected* story is an unfinished story whose readiness reasons name the decision; it is *held* when todo and *under way* when started. `needed_in` (this sprint, next sprint, beyond, none) says when a decision is needed, the *next step* what it needs, and the *lane* where the queue lists it; all three are derived on read and never stored (kb/ADR-0014 R6, kb/ADR-0027 R1). The queue orders entries by lane, then group (decisions that hold work above those that hold none), then urgency.

- **Initiative, epic, story.** The three levels of the hierarchy; only stories are delivered. Keys come from one sequence (`ABC-12`, kb/ADR-0002 R5). An initiative has a **horizon** (a configured bucket) and an optional target month.
- **Kind.** A story is a `story`, a `bug` or a `chore`. A story of kind `story` needs an epic; an **untriaged** story is one of kind `story` with no epic (`Story::is_untriaged`).
- **Delivery state and resolution.** `todo`, `in_progress`, `in_review`, `testing`, `done`; a story that enters `done` carries a **resolution**, `completed` or `dropped`.
- **Blocker.** A story that blocks another until it is merged (testing or done); a started one never blocks its own owner's dependents (kb/ADR-0027 R5).
- **Readiness.** A story is ready or it has reasons; computed on read, never stored (kb/ADR-0027 R1). A **discovery mark** on an initiative or epic says its discovery is done.
- **Override.** An interactive caller's written reason for entering active work although the story is not ready (kb/ADR-0027 R2). **Grandfathered** stories are imported already in their state and are never overrides.
- **Required decision.** A decision a work item waits on, with a **decider**, a due date and an optional **reversibility** (`one_way` or `two_way`, kb/ADR-0013). It points at a **record reference** (repository, path, record id) in a household repository. Its **check result** is `unchecked`, `accepted`, `not_accepted`, `proposed_pr_open` or `not_found`. It ends with a **closure kind**, `decided` or `withdrawn`.
- **Sprint, goal, goal link, upkeep line.** A sprint is a draft (the next sprint: no window, at most one), active (a window of time from its opening) or closed; a goal is a team intention with confidence and verdict, a goal link ties a goal to an initiative, epic or story (`link_goal`; `sprint::serves` says which goals a story serves, through itself, its epic or its epic's initiative; kb/ADR-0014 R3), and the upkeep line is the configured reference for how much of a sprint is upkeep (kb/ADR-0014). A *candidate* is a draft's goal: a role in conversation and in kb/ADR-0026, not bound vocabulary; the API and the UI say goal.
- **Urgent mark, urgent, war room.** A person marks one initiative, epic or story **urgent**, with an optional reason; the mark lasts one sprint length unless renewed, and marking again replaces it. While a mark is active its item, the item's ancestors and its descendants are urgent (a done story is not, and a mark on a done story makes nothing urgent); urgency is computed on read, never stored. The **war room** lists every stored mark, active then expired, until a person clears or renews it (kb/ADR-0017).
- **Attachment.** An image (PNG, JPEG or WebP) owned by one initiative, epic or story, shown inline in its Markdown and listed under the item. It is uploaded first and owned by none until the write that saves the text showing it claims it (kb/ADR-0022 R2); only its uploader sees it until then. The UI says image for what a person adds and attachment for what an item owns; it never says unclaimed.
- **Promise, effective date, holder, state, overdue, late, due.** A person **promises** a story or an epic by a calendar date, with an optional short recipient; initiatives are not promised and agents cannot promise (kb/ADR-0025 R1, R2). A story's **effective date** is the earlier of its own promise and the one it gets from its epic, which applies to each story not done when it was set (R3); the **holder** says whose date it is: the story or the epic. A story's promise **state** is derived on read from the effective date, its delivery and the request's UTC date: `open`; `overdue` (unfinished after the date, and it keeps its date until a person changes it); `kept` (done on or before the date); `late` (done after it); `dropped` (resolved dropped); an epic has no state of its own and a done story without `done_at` has none (R4). An open promise is **due** `this_sprint`, `next_sprint` or `later` by one function over the active and draft sprints' planned `ends_on` (R5). An epic's own promise is **past** once its date is before today: a word for display (the epic chip's "Past 20 Nov", shown with the overdue tone and clock; `past` on the wire), not a state; R4 gives an epic none. **A sprint's promise range** is R8's: the dates after the previous sprint's planned `ends_on` and up to its own (`sprint_range`; `promises_after` on the wire; a draft has none), and a sprint's promises are the stories whose effective date is in it. "Window" stays with a sprint's opening-to-closing span and with the decision queue: `promise::window` (and `PromiseWindow`, `StoryPromise::window`) is the window a promise asks a blocking decision for, as `queue.rs` calls a story's `NeededIn` its window; it was renamed from `lane` so as not to collide with `queue::lane`. Nothing is stored but the date, who set it, when and the recipient. There is no "slipped" and no "broken".
- **Due this sprint and a sprint's promise range differ.** R5's "due this sprint" has no lower bound (any open date up to the active sprint's planned end), while R8's range starts after the previous sprint's planned end. They agree while sprints close on their planned ends; when a sprint closes on or before its planned end, an open promise dated up to that end still reads "· this sprint" and matches `promised=this_sprint`, while only the closed sprint lists it. Both follow kb/ADR-0025 as written; which one the active sprint's list shows is settled in GRA-610, before `GET /sprints` fills it.
- **Client affordances that copy a server rule on purpose.** The promise dialog refuses a past date before sending, offers a sprint shortcut only while that sprint's planned end is not past, limits the recipient to 200 characters (counted in code points, as the server counts), and notes when the epic's promise is earlier (`epicNote`, its words pinned in `promise.fixture.json`); the board filters stories by `promised` on the client with `promisedMatches`, whose truth table is that fixture's `filter` rows, which `Filter::matches`'s test reads too. Each is a UX affordance so the person sees the problem before submitting; the server re-checks every one (`change`, `shortcut`, `MAX_RECIPIENT`, `story_promise`), and its answer wins. A shortcut is sent only while the end it filled is still the end offered for it; once the end moves or the sprint closes under the open dialog, the date in the field is sent instead.

## Aggregates

Each aggregate is a Rust type under `crates/reeve-planning/src/domain/`, persisted by one port in `src/ports/`. Rules live on the aggregate or beside it in `domain/`; the handler loads, calls, persists and writes the activity entry. Reason: a rule on the aggregate is testable without a database. For moves and decisions the aggregate is the only way in (private fields, `Story::move_to`, `RequiredDecision`'s methods), so a second caller cannot skip the rule. Item edits are not there yet: `edit_story` checks the title (`check_title`) and the criteria in the handler, `edit_epic` and `edit_initiative` validate through `Epic::new` and `Initiative::new`, and all three rebuild the item with `from_persisted` before `update_*` writes it, so a new edit path must repeat those checks.

| Aggregate | Owns | Port |
|---|---|---|
| `Initiative` | title, brief, horizon, target month, discovery mark | `InitiativeRepo` |
| `Epic` | title, brief, parent initiative, discovery mark | `EpicRepo` |
| `Story` | kind, description, criteria, owner, labels, rank, delivery, resolution | `StoryRepo` |
| `RequiredDecision` | planner fields, record reference, PR url, check, closure | `DecisionRepo` |
| `Sprint` | window and state | `SprintRepo` |
| `Goal` | outcome, check, owner, notes, confidence and verdict, and its sprint, changed only by an opening | `SprintRepo` |
| `UrgentMark` | time, person and optional reason of the mark on one item | `UrgencyRepo` |
| `Promise` (not part of `Story` or `Epic`) | date, setter, time and optional recipient on one story or epic | `PromiseRepo` |
| `Attachment` | owner (one item, or none), uploader, filename, type, size and hash; its bytes as 1 MiB chunks | `AttachmentRepo` |

`Sprint` and `Goal` are two aggregates behind one port: each has its own version and is locked (`sprint_for_update`, `goal_for_update`) and saved (`save_sprint`, `save_goal`) on its own. A `GoalLink` (goal id and item key) is a row of its own, written by `insert_goal_link` and `delete_goal_link`, not a field of `Goal`. Blocking edges are not part of `Story`: they are rows written by `StoryRepo::add_block` and `remove_block` and read as readiness input (`ReadinessInput::blockers`, each with its delivery and owner, plus the story's `owner`; `domain/readiness.rs`'s `blocks` decides which count, kb/ADR-0027 R5). Comments have no domain type: `CommentRepo` inserts them (`insert_comment`, `insert_item_comment`) and returns `CommentDto` rows. The "no DTO" guarantee below covers `domain/` only: some ports still carry wire types (`CommentRepo`'s `CommentDto`, `GoalVia` in `PlanningReads::active_goals_of_stories`). A pull request (`domain/pull_request.rs`, `PullRequestRepo`) is a cache of GitHub's state, not an aggregate with business rules. An urgent mark is stored in its item's row (`urgent_at`, `urgent_by`, `urgent_reason`) but is no field of `Initiative`, `Epic` or `Story`: only `UrgencyRepo` reads or writes those columns, and no item `UPDATE` names them, so marking takes no version and no edit or move drops a mark (kb/ADR-0017 R1, R5). A promise sits beside `Story` and `Epic` the same way: four columns on the item's row, no field of either aggregate, written only through `PromiseRepo` (`set_promise`, `clear_promise`, after `lock_for_promise`'s `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`) without taking or changing the item's version. Its rules are pure and live in `domain/promise.rs`.

### Invariants and where they live

- **The delivery rule is `Story::move_to`** (`domain/items.rs`): entering `done` needs a resolution and a completed story needs an active origin, re-resolving a done story follows the same rule (kb/ADR-0002 R4), a not-ready entry into active work is refused, and an override needs an interactive caller and a non-blank reason (kb/ADR-0027 R2). It returns `Moved::Unchanged` or `Moved::Moved { from, override_reason }`, or a `MoveRefused`, and leaves the story untouched on a refusal. It also sets the owner when a story without one enters any active state (kb/ADR-0027 R6); the gate judges such a story as the mover's (`readiness_in`'s `judge_as`). It receives `ready: bool` and nothing from sprints or goals. Reason: readiness is a derived fact the handler loads (kb/ADR-0027 R1), and a goal must never decide a move (kb/ADR-0014 R4).
- **Decision rules are `RequiredDecision::{link, edit, record_check, close}`** (`domain/decision.rs`), all refusing with `DecisionError` and leaving the decision untouched. `link` resets the check to `unchecked` (kb/ADR-0004 R3); `edit` changes only title, decider, due and reversibility and returns the typed `FieldChange`s; `record_check` refuses, in order, no reference, a result for a changed reference (a conflict at the API), and a closed decision, and reports `CheckOutcome::Unchanged` for a result that says the same; `close` needs a reason, and a one-way decision cannot be closed as `decided` (`OneWayDecided`). Input is validated by value objects built before any row is read: `RecordRef::new`, `PrUrl::parse`, `DecisionEdit::new`.
- **A changed horizon must be a configured bucket** (`check_horizon_change`, `domain/items.rs`); an unchanged horizon is always valid, so unlisting a bucket does not block editing the initiative's other fields. Create and import check the bucket in `Initiative::new`.
- **A sprint allows a write only in some states** (`sprint::allow` over `Op`, `domain/sprint.rs`): one explicit table, so a new state or operation fails to compile (kb/ADR-0014 R1, R6).
- **Urgency is `urgency` over the stored marks and their `Lineage`** (`domain/urgency.rs`): a mark is active until `expires_at`, its time plus the configured sprint length (kb/ADR-0017 R2); an active mark on a story reaches its epic and initiative, on an epic its initiative and its unfinished stories, on an initiative its epics and their unfinished stories, never siblings, and never further from a derived urgency (R3). The handler computes it once per snapshot (`urgency_in`, `handler/urgent.rs`) and every item DTO carries it; nothing stores it (R1). Marking, renewing and clearing need an interactive caller (`interactive`, `handler/mod.rs`), are logged `urgent_marked` and `urgent_cleared`, never `override` (R4), and a reason is trimmed and at most 200 characters (`clean_reason`). The rules on the mark itself are still in the handler and the adapter's SQL; they move onto the domain when next touched.
- **Attachment rules are pure** (`domain/attachment.rs`): `ImageType::sniff` names the type by the bytes, `strip_metadata` removes EXIF, XMP, IPTC, comments, MPF, JFIF thumbnails, trailers, PNG text and what animation frames carry besides their bitstream, without touching image data, and keeps only a non-upright orientation; `clean_filename` and the 10 MiB cap (kb/ADR-0023 R1–R3). A person may hold at most 200 MiB of unclaimed uploads (`MAX_UNCLAIMED_BYTES`). The claim is one conditional `UPDATE` in the adapter, inside the saving write's transaction (`claim_attachments`, `handler/attachments.rs`): the caller's unclaimed uploads become the item's, ones already the item's and ones gone are ignored, anything else refuses the whole write. An upload, a claim and a delete never take or change the item's version; `attachment_added` and `attachment_removed` are logged on the item, and an unclaimed upload is never logged (kb/ADR-0023 R7).
- **Promise rules are pure** (`domain/promise.rs`): the effective date, the state, the due period and a sprint's promise range are functions of the stored facts and a `Clock` (today and the active and draft sprints' planned `ends_on`). `Clock::at(now, sprints)` is its one assembly: `promise_clock_in` (`handler/promise.rs`) builds it once per read snapshot from `SprintRepo::active_and_draft_in`, and the sprint views from the sprints they have already read; it travels beside the urgency into the story and epic assemblers, and every view, filter and queue rule classifies through it (kb/ADR-0025 R4, R5, R8). `StoryPromise` (private fields; `story_promise` is its only constructor) says whose promise holds as `HeldBy` (`Own` or `Epic(key)`), with that promise always present. Who may be promised is `Promisable` (`Story(delivery)` or `Epic`; never an initiative, R1): `change` takes it, refuses a done story (R2), and returns None when nothing changes (the same date and recipient); the port takes `PromiseItem` (`Story` or `Epic`), and the handler refuses an initiative before touching it. `sprint_range` selects R8's range, the draft rules included. The decision queue reads the domain value (`PromiseWindow::of`), never the wire DTO. Setting, changing and clearing need an interactive caller and are logged under their own actions, `promised` and `promise_cleared`, never `override` (R2).
- **Readiness is `compute_readiness` over a `ReadinessInput`** (`domain/readiness.rs`). The only derivation stored is a decision's check result, because it needs git.
- **The time facts `started_at` and `done_at` are set by `set_delivery`'s SQL** from the row's state, not by the aggregate. This is the current implementation choice, not a limit of the aggregate: `move_story` reads the story `FOR UPDATE` (`get_story_for_update`) before it calls `move_to`, so the story it holds has the locked state. The same SQL keeps a stored owner (`COALESCE(owner, …)`), so an owner change must not rely on `set_delivery`.
- **Fields are private.** `Story`, `RequiredDecision` and `RecordRef` expose getters and `from_persisted` for the adapter and the integration tests. Reason: a field that can be assigned bypasses the rule that guards it. `from_persisted` does not validate, so it is still a way around the rules: the item edit handlers use it, as above.

## The read side

Reads that join other aggregates' tables are on `PlanningReads`: `readiness_inputs` (the one five-statement loader), counts, the decision queue's inputs (`draft_goals_of_stories`, `decision_lineage_in`), search, the export readers and `urgency_lineage_in` (the epics and stories an urgent mark reaches), and the promise reads `promise_facts_in` and `promised_between_in`. The clock's active and draft sprints (`active_and_draft_in`) read only sprints, so they are on `SprintRepo`. `KeyRepo` holds the one key sequence and every key or external-reference lookup across item types. Reason: a read across aggregates belongs to no single aggregate, and one sequence keeps keys unique across types.

Wire DTOs are built only in `handler/dto.rs`; `domain/` returns domain values (`Summary`, `QueueStory`, `QueueEntry`, `QueueSummary`; `AffectedStory` is the classifier's input). Reason: the domain must not change when the wire does, and the OpenAPI snapshot catches the wire side. Values are pinned by `crates/reeve-planning/tests/dto_values.rs`.

## What the context never does

Each guarantee is pinned by `scripts/check-context-isolation.sh`, proven by its fixtures in `scripts/tests/isolation.sh` (`mise run isolation-test`), or by a named test.

- **It never stores readiness** (kb/ADR-0027 R1). Pinned by the schema, which has no readiness column; the gate itself is pinned by `leaving_done_for_an_active_state_is_gated_by_readiness` in `crates/reeve-planning/tests/rules.rs`.
- **It never moves a story outside `move_story`** (kb/ADR-0002 R4). The lint refuses the names `set_delivery` and `move_to` in any handler file except `handler/moves.rs`, whether called, called through UFCS or taken as a method value. The lint is a tripwire over source text, not a proof (a call through another handler method or a macro escapes it); the behaviour is pinned by `crates/reeve-planning/tests/rules.rs`.
- **It never reads sprints, goals or urgency to move a story** (kb/ADR-0014 R4, kb/ADR-0017 R5). The lint refuses `ports.sprints`, `active_goals_of_stories`, `draft_goals_of_stories`, `decision_lineage_in`, `decision_queue(_in)(`, `decision_context(`, `ports.urgency`, `urgency_lineage_in` and `urgency_in` in `handler/moves.rs`. Test: `urgency_changes_no_readiness_delivery_or_progress` in `crates/reeve-planning/tests/handlers.rs`.
- **It never stores urgency, and a mark never changes its item** (kb/ADR-0017 R1, R5). Tests: `urgent_marks_round_trip_without_touching_the_item` in `crates/reeve-planning/tests/repo.rs` and `a_mark_never_conflicts_with_an_edit_or_a_move_and_survives_both` in `crates/reeve-planning/tests/handlers.rs`.
- **It never stores a promise state, and a promise never gates** (kb/ADR-0025 R1, R7, R8). Kept, late, overdue, dropped and due are derived on read; delivery validation, readiness, progress and rank ignore promises; a promise never writes a decision's due date (`only_unfinished_promises_pull_decisions` in `crates/reeve-planning/tests/handlers.rs`). `a_promise_survives_edits_moves_and_the_discovery_mark` in the same file pins that no edit or move drops a promise. The lint refuses `ports.promises`, `promise_facts_in`, `promised_between_in`, `promise_clock_in`, `active_and_draft_in` and `set_promise`/`clear_promise` (and their `_in` forms) in `handler/moves.rs`. Tests: `promises_change_no_readiness_delivery_progress_or_rank` in `crates/reeve-planning/tests/handlers.rs`.
- **The importer and the webhook never read or write a promise** (kb/ADR-0025 R2). Tests: `the_importer_has_no_path_to_a_promise` in `crates/reeve-server/tests/import_export.rs` and `webhook_never_touches_a_promise` in `crates/reeve-server/tests/webhook.rs`.
- **A goal changes sprint only when its draft opens** (`open_sprint_leaving`, `handler/sprints.rs`; the handler sorts and dedups the ids and refuses the first that is not the draft's): the goals left out move to the next draft by one conditional `UPDATE` constrained by the source sprint (`SprintRepo::move_goals`), without a version, the same kind of exception as `claim_attachments`; an active or closed sprint's goals never move (kb/ADR-0026). Every goal write therefore compares the locked goal's sprint with the locked sprint before the sprint's state (`goal_locked`, which `unlink_goal` uses too), and answers Conflict with current -1 (concurrent update, retry) when they differ. The lint refuses `move_goals` (the port call and the repo fn) in every planning handler but `handler/sprints.rs`. Tests: `opening_leaves_chosen_goals_in_the_next_draft`, `opening_refuses_leaving_out_a_goal_it_does_not_hold` and the three `…_when_an_opening_moved_its_goal_meanwhile` tests in `crates/reeve-planning/tests/sprints.rs`.
- **The GitHub webhook never moves a story.** The lint allows `handler/prs.rs` only `ports.pull_requests.<method>` and `ports.stories.get_story` (no alias of a port, no UFCS) and refuses `move_story`; `recording_a_pull_request_links_updates_and_never_moves_the_story` in `crates/reeve-planning/tests/handlers.rs` pins the behaviour; `PullRequestRepo` has no method that writes delivery, resolution or version. Tests: `webhook_never_moves_a_story_that_could_move`, `the_webhook_has_no_path_to_moving_a_story` and `webhook_never_touches_an_urgent_mark` in `crates/reeve-server/tests/webhook.rs`.
- **The import never writes an `override` entry.** Grandfathered stories are created in their state and logged `imported`. Test: `import_creates_with_state_once_and_never_logs_an_override` in `crates/reeve-planning/tests/handlers.rs`, and the server's `crates/reeve-server/tests/import_export.rs`, where `the_importer_has_no_path_to_an_urgent_mark` also pins that the import never reads or writes a mark (kb/ADR-0017 R5).
- **The domain never names a wire DTO.** The lint refuses any identifier ending in `Dto`, and `StoryWithReadiness`, under `domain/`.
- **It never makes an attachment a readiness fact, and an attachment never moves a story or changes its version.** Tests: `deletes_log_on_the_owner_only_when_claimed_and_leave_versions` in `crates/reeve-planning/tests/attachment_handlers.rs`.
- **Only an interactive caller overrides.** `move_to` refuses automation with `AutomationCannotOverride`; tests in `crates/reeve-planning/tests/rules.rs` and `handlers.rs` (`only_interactive_callers_may_override_a_system_actor_is_refused`).

A use case that spans ports (a move reads readiness, the story and the activity log) runs in one transaction that the handler opens and the port methods borrow. Reason: kb/ADR-0010 R5.

## Context map

- **Identity (upstream, authenticates).** Planning's `Ctx { actor, via, now }` is its own type; the server builds it from identity's authenticated token (`crates/reeve-server/src/auth/mod.rs`). Planning never imports identity. Reason: kb/ADR-0010, the supplier can change without planning noticing.
- **Activity (downstream, implements planning's port).** `reeve-activity` implements `ActivitySink`; planning calls `record` in the mutation's transaction.
- **Jira (anticorruption layer in the CLI).** The translation from Jira's status to delivery, type to kind and owner mapping is `crates/reeve/src/jira/`. Planning's import endpoints receive planner-vocabulary items and create them with their state. Planning knows nothing of Jira beyond an opaque external reference.
- **Decision records (outside planning).** Records live in household repositories. The CLI's scheduled check reads them and posts check results; it is the only authority that does (kb/ADR-0004, kb/ADR-0016). Planning stores the reference and the result and never reads a repository. Only an admin's check stores `accepted`.
- **GitHub (informational).** The webhook upserts a story's pull request through `PullRequestRepo` as a cache; status never decides delivery.
